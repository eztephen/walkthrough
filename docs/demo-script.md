# Demo script — three minutes, built around the timer

The whole pitch is one number: **how long their report takes by hand**, set against **how long it just took on your phone**. Everything below exists to get both numbers in front of them without you having to argue.

---

## Before you walk in

- **Phone charged, demo link open, signed in to nothing.** Use the deployed URL, not localhost.
- **Know their portfolio size** from their website. You'll use it for pricing.
- **Have your price ready**, written down. Don't open with it.
- **Refresh the page just before you hand the phone over.** The timer starts when the page loads, and nothing is saved — a refresh gives you a clean inspection with the sample rooms back in place.

---

## The three minutes

### 0:00 – 0:30 · Ask first

> "Before I show you anything — how long does a routine inspection report take you? From walking in the door to the owner having it in their inbox."

**Write their answer down where they can see you do it.** That number is the whole pitch. If they say "about two hours," you are going to point at it in two and a half minutes.

### 0:30 – 1:00 · Show the finished thing

Tap **View owner report** on the pre-loaded sample.

> "This is what the owner gets."

Let them scroll. Don't narrate every section. Point at one thing only — the **maintenance schedule** — and say:

> "Those go straight to your tradies as quote requests."

Tap **← Back to inspection**, then refresh the page so the timer restarts from zero.

### 1:00 – 2:30 · Hand them the phone

> "Inspect this room."

Open **Bathroom** for them, then let go of the phone. Tell them to pretend it's the office they're sitting in:

1. Tap a condition.
2. Type one line of notes.
3. Tap **Add photo** — the camera opens — and photograph the room.
4. Add one maintenance item: *"blind cord broken"*, *"scuff on the wall"*, whatever is actually in front of them.

**Stay quiet.** Don't reach for the phone. If they get stuck, point — don't take over. The moment they realise they did it themselves is the moment that sells it.

### 2:30 – 3:00 · The number

Have them tap **Generate owner report**. The green banner says *"Report generated in 2:10"* (or whatever it is).

Point at the banner. Then point at the number you wrote down.

> "You said two hours."

Then stop talking. Let them scroll their own report — their room, their photo, their maintenance item. Let them tap **Send quote requests**.

The silence after this is the most important part of the demo. Don't fill it.

---

## Questions they'll ask — and honest answers

**"What does it cost?"**
Say it plainly, once: *"For the first agencies I work with, it's {setup fee} to set it up with your templates, then {price} per property a month."* Don't discount on the spot. If they push, ask how many properties they manage and do the maths in front of them against the hours they told you.

**"Does it work without signal?"**
*"Not yet. How often are you on an inspection without signal?"* Write the answer down — that's a feature request with a priority attached.

**"Does it connect to our property management software?"**
*"Not yet. Which one do you use?"* Write it down. If three agencies name the same system, that's your next build.

**"Can it do entry and exit inspections?"**
*"It does routine inspections today. What's different about your entry reports?"* Their answer is your spec.

**"Where does the data go?"**
*"In this demo, nowhere — it only exists in this browser tab, and it's gone when you close it. The version you'd use stores it in {region}, and I'll send you exactly how and where in writing before you put a real tenant's details in it."* Then actually do that. Tenant data is personal information, and in New Zealand the Privacy Act 2020 applies to how it's stored and who can see it.

**"What happens if you stop running it?"**
*"Every report is a PDF you keep. You'd lose the tool, not the reports."* Make sure that stays true.

---

## Don't

- **Don't demo anything that isn't built.** "It'll do that soon" loses more deals than "it doesn't do that."
- **Don't apologise for what's missing.** Answer the question, write down the request, move on.
- **Don't start on the login screen or a slide.** Start with the finished report.
- **Don't talk while they're using it.**
- **Don't leave without a next step** — a trial date, a second meeting with the principal, or a clear no. "I'll think about it" with no date is a no.

---

## Afterwards — same day

1. **Send them their own report.** On your phone, tap **Download / print PDF**, save it, and email it: *"Here's the report you made of your office in two minutes."* It's the best follow-up you'll ever send, because it's theirs.
2. **Write down the five things they said unprompted.** Across five demos, the requests that repeat are your roadmap. The ones that don't are noise.
3. **Log it in the outreach sheet** with the next step and its date.
